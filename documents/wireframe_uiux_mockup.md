# Wireframe & UI/UX Mock Up - SWAYOG Customer Mobile App

## 1. Design Principles (Premium Solar Theme)
The application leverages a dark-mode first design to reflect modern clean energy styling.
* **Background Dark**: `#0B132B`
* **Surface Dark**: `#1C2541`
* **Primary Solar Orange**: `#FF6B00`
* **Eco Green**: `#10B981`
* **Info Blue**: `#0284C7`
